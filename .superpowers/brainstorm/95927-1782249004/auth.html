How do users sign up?

Auth method affects friction, trust, and how roommates identify each other in the app.

A

Apple / Google Sign-In

One tap, no password. Native feel on iOS. Auto-fills display name and avatar.

Lowest friction. Required by App Store if you offer any social login. Best for college users who already have Apple/Google accounts.

B

Phone Number (OTP)

Enter phone number, verify with a code. No password needed.

Very common in social apps (WhatsApp, Splitwise). Easy to identify roommates. Costs $$ per SMS at scale.

C

Email + Password

Classic sign-up. Familiar but highest friction.

No extra cost. Works everywhere. But college users bounce on email forms — feels dated for a consumer app.

D

Apple/Google + Email fallback

Social login first, email as backup for web or Android users without Google.

Recommended. Covers iOS native feel + web accessibility requirement.

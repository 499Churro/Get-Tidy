What does a chore need?

When someone creates a chore, what information should they fill in? Less is more — every optional field adds friction. Select what's required, leave out what's noise.

A

Name REQUIRED

"Vacuum living room", "Take out trash", "Clean bathroom"

B

Assigned to

Which roommate is responsible. Can be left unassigned (shared task).

C

Due date

When it needs to be done by. Enables due-today and overdue notifications.

D

Notes

Optional context — "use the blue mop", "before Sunday dinner".

E

Photo proof on completion

Roommate must attach a photo to mark a chore done. Accountability layer.
